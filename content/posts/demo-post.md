DARTS begins as a personal project.

Its goal is to work on the development of artificial intelligence, especially in the field of **physical AI**: systems that act in real environments, deal with uncertainty, and must make decisions beyond controlled demos.

That focus matters. A large part of today's AI conversation is centered on classical LLMs and other more passive systems. Those tools are useful, but they are not the whole picture. I am especially interested in intelligence that has to **perceive, move, adapt, and operate in the physical world**.

DARTS is public for two reasons.

The first is to make the work visible: to share ideas, experiments, progress, and failures in an open way.

The second is to **encourage other people to contribute**. If this direction resonates with you, whether from robotics, machine learning, simulation, control, systems, or applied research, you are welcome here.

This project starts small, but it is built with the intention of growing through collaboration.

If DARTS can help connect people interested in physical AI and push real-world intelligence a bit further, then making it public is already worth it.
