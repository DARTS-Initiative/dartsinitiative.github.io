# First Steps of the DARTS Initiative

We are excited to share the initial progress of the DARTS project. Our goal is to create an open ecosystem for the development of autonomous drone swarms.

![DARTS Swarm Technology](/images/drone-swarm.png)

## Why DARTS?

In the current landscape of robotics, **scalability** and **resilience** are often limiting factors. DARTS aims to address these by:

1.  **Autonomous Coordination**: Intelligent algorithms for decentralized decision-making.
2.  **Resilient Communication**: Robust links even in challenging environments.
3.  **Scalable Architecture**: Support for swarms of various sizes.

> "The future of robotics is not in isolated units, but in the power of the collective." — *DARTS Team*
