# Welcome to DARTS

We are thrilled to announce the official launch of the **DARTS Initiative** (Drone Autonomous, Resilient, and Scalable Swarms). This project represents a bold step towards a future where autonomous agents work together to solve complex problems in real-world environments.

## Our Vision

At DARTS, we believe that the next generation of robotics isn't about single, complex machines, but about the power of **collective intelligence**. By focusing on decentralized coordination and robust communication, we aim to create swarms that are more than the sum of their parts.

## Why Open Source?

The challenges of drone swarm technology—ranging from obstacle avoidance to dynamic networking—are too large for any single entity to solve alone. That's why DARTS is built on a foundation of **open source principles**. We want researchers, developers, and enthusiasts from around the world to contribute, experiment, and grow this ecosystem with us.

## What's Next?

In the coming months, we will be sharing:
- **Core Algorithms**: Our vision for decentralized swarm control.
- **Hardware Specifications**: Open designs for resilient drone platforms.
- **Development Tools**: Libraries and simulators to accelerate your research.

> "The strength of the swarm is the drone, and the strength of the drone is the swarm."

We are just getting started. If you're interested in robotics, AI, or decentralized systems, we'd love to have you on board.
