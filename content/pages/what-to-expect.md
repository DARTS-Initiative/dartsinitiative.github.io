DARTS is built around open work. Everything developed here is shared publicly 
as it happens: the ideas, the process, the decisions, and the results.

This is what you will find here.

## Projects

All projects developed under DARTS are open source. This includes code 
repositories, utilities, simulation environments, and models. Everything 
is accessible directly through our GitHub organization and, where relevant, 
through Hugging Face.

## Content

**DARTS updates** — Decisions about the direction of the initiative, new 
collaborators, new projects, and anything else that shapes what DARTS is becoming.

**Project updates** — As projects move forward, we document them. Not just 
the milestones, but the decisions behind them: what was tried, what the results 
looked like, and what comes next.

**Ecosystem** — Relevant developments in the broader research landscape, 
analyzed through the lens of what DARTS is actively working on. Not a 
summary of the field, but an assessment of what matters for the problems 
we are trying to solve.
